| Asset | Source / purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `images/team.webp` | Unsplash photo `photo-1522071820081-009f0129c71c`, temporary team/collaboration photo: https://unsplash.com/photos/people-sitting-near-table-with-laptop-computer-QBpZGqEMsKg |

Photographs are illustrative and do not represent the actual team or office. Source download URLs live in `scripts/download-assets.mjs`. Images use local WebP files and Next Image optimization rather than third-party requests at page view time.

`images/product/{dashboard,customers,records,users,plans}.svg` are original lightweight vector interface illustrations generated by `scripts/generate-product-previews.mjs`. All people, counts, prices and records in them are fictional demo data. Replace them with real screenshots through the five `PREVIEW_*_IMAGE` environment values.

To replace images, add real assets under `public/images/`, change `TEAM_IMAGE` and `STORY_IMAGE` in `.env.local`, update alt text where the image content changes, and rebuild. Set `COMPANY_LOGO` to a local logo asset and replace the favicon separately.
